FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about AxleIQ.

What is AxleIQ?

AxleIQ is a software company building focused, affordable tools for commercial transportation. Its current product family includes AxleIQ Collect and AxleIQ Fleet.

What is AxleIQ Collect?

AxleIQ Collect is a professional field reporting and evidence management platform for commercial vehicle enforcement.

What is AxleIQ Fleet?

AxleIQ Fleet is a developing digital vehicle-reporting platform for drivers, owner-operators, and small fleets. It is currently positioned for beta testing.

Where can I download the apps?

Store links will be added as each product becomes broadly available. Contact us for current testing and availability information.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@getaxleiq.com with the product name, device type, a short description, and screenshots when available.
