A PURPOSE-BUILT DAILY WORKFLOW

Simple for the driver. Useful for the manager.

Fleet keeps the reporting experience focused so the work gets completed consistently without slowing down the start or end of a shift.

01

Select the vehicle

The driver signs in and chooses the assigned vehicle from a clean, mobile-first list.

02

Complete the guided report

Move through a short pre-trip or post-trip checklist designed to take roughly 30–60 seconds when no defect is found.

03

Document defects clearly

Add required photos, notes, and severity details only when an issue needs attention.

04

Review and sign

Confirm the report and add the driver signature before submission.

05

Give managers visibility

Submitted reports appear in the manager portal with vehicle, driver, defect, and completion status.

06

Keep dependable records

Maintain organized report history for follow-up, documentation, and day-to-day fleet oversight.

THE AXLEIQ FLEET STANDARD

No bloat. No maze. No unnecessary setup.

Fleet is being built around three priorities that matter every day.

Fast adoption

Drivers should understand the workflow with little or no training.

Consistent completion

The system should make the right process the easiest process.

Clear accountability

Managers should be able to see completion, defects, and follow-up without digging.
